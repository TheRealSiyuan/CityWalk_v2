## Description

Swing through a hand-built, storybook London.

Hold **Swing** and you're away: grab a rooftop, let the arc carry you, and the next rope
catches you on its own. Let go at the top for big air. Run along the Embankment, hop across
terraces, zip up to a ledge, and drop onto the roof of St Paul's.

**Find all 13 landmarks** — Big Ben, the London Eye, Westminster Abbey, Buckingham Palace,
Trafalgar Square, the British Museum, St Paul's, the Millennium Bridge, Tate Modern, the
Shard, the Tower of London, Tower Bridge and the BT Tower — and learn one true fact at each.

**Race the city.** Three timed landmark routes — Westminster Dash, River Run and City Sprint.
Follow the beacon, beat your best time.

**Bring friends.** Press Share and anyone who opens your link appears beside you (up to 8).

### Controls
| | Phone | Keyboard + mouse |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Drag left side (further = run) | WASD (Shift = walk) |
| Look | Drag right side | Click, then move mouse |
| Jump | Jump (again in mid-air) | Space (again in mid-air) |
| Swing | Hold Swing — keep holding to chain | Hold click or E |

### Notes
- Runs in any modern browser; no download, no account.
- Progress and best times are saved on your device only.
- Multiplayer is peer-to-peer: players in the same room connect directly, which means they
  can see each other's IP address, as in most peer-to-peer games. There is no chat.
- Made with Three.js. Everything — buildings, character, sounds — is generated in code.
