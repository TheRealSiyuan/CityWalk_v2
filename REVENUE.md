## Planning scenarios (assumptions, not forecasts)

There is no public data on donation rates for free browser games on itch.io, and
CrazyGames publishes no ad rates. The numbers below are explicit assumptions to show the
shape of the problem; replace them with measured figures as soon as any exist.

Net per itch.io donation at the default 10% share: roughly **$2.31 on a $3 tip** ($0.30 to
itch.io, then $0.30 + 2.9% processing), before payout fees.

| Monthly plays | Assumed donor rate | Avg tip | Approx. net / month |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | 0.2% | $3 | ~$5 |
| 10,000 | 0.2% | $3 | ~$46 |
| 10,000 | 0.5% | $4 | ~$159 |

Takeaways:

- Donations alone will not reach £1,000/month without very large audiences. They are the
  fastest way to the *first* £1–£100, not a business.
- Sustainable income needs advertising share at volume (CrazyGames Full Launch) and/or a
  paid product people come back for. Both depend on retention, which is what to measure next.
